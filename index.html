Questions

Is it really free?

Yes. Every preset, font and export is free, with no account, no watermark and no download limit. The site is paid for by the ads you see and by an optional wallpaper pack.

What size should a Notion cover be?

Notion recommends 1500 × 600 pixels, which is exactly what this tool exports. Phones and the Notion app show a narrower crop, so keep text near the centre.

Do my images or text get uploaded anywhere?

No. The PNG is drawn in your browser with the Canvas API. Nothing you type is sent to a server.

Can I use the images commercially?

Yes. Whatever you generate here is yours, including in client workspaces and paid templates. No credit needed.

Can I edit the text later?

A PNG is flat, so re-open the tool and make a new one. Your last settings are remembered in your browser, so it takes a few seconds.

Which fonts are included?

Inter, Playfair Display, Lora and Space Mono — all open-licensed and loaded from Google Fonts. Add more by editing the font list in app.js.
